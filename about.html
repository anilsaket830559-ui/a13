About WristCo

An independent watch site for people who want the mechanism explained, not the marketing repeated.

WristCo started because specification sheets are published in full and explained almost nowhere. Lug-to-lug decides whether a watch fits. Beat rate decides how often it needs servicing. Neither appears in most reviews.

What we publish

Two long guides and three free tools. How Watches Work covers movements, dimensions and complications. The Care & Service Guide covers daily handling, magnetism, water and servicing. The tools answer the three questions we were asked most while writing them.

How we stay independent

We take no payment, lent watches or review samples from brands or retailers, and there are no affiliate links anywhere on this site. We write about mechanisms rather than models, which keeps the temptation away. If ads appear, a third party serves them and they have no influence on what we publish.

What we are not

We are not a repairer, a valuer or an authentication service, and nothing here is a price guide. Watches are not treated on this site as a way to make money, and we do not comment on resale values. If you need work done, find an accredited watchmaker.

How we work

Our guidance comes from manufacturers’ technical documentation, published horological reference material, conversations with watchmakers and long-term ownership. Where the right answer depends on your particular movement or case, we say so. Our Editorial Policy explains how we research, review and correct what we publish.

Corrections

If a tool gives you an odd result or a guide contradicts your watchmaker, write to hello@wristco.com or use the contact page.
